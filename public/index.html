RULES

How to play

  • Sink every ship by firing at grid squares. A hit resets consecutive misses.
  • Large ships occupy 3 cells and show blue; small ships occupy 2 cells and show orange.
  • Easy has unlimited misses; Normal ends after 5 consecutive misses; Hard ends after 3. In Normal and Hard, the counter below the board shows consecutive misses and how many remain before defeat; a hit resets it.
  • A loss or restarting an unfinished mission resets your win streak. Choose your mode before building a streak; it locks while a streak is active.
  • Win streak rewards: a flare at 3 wins reveals a large-ship piece; a pirate at 5 reveals a small-ship piece.
  • At 6 wins and every win after that, earn a cannon shot. On a 6×6 board it fires at two squares. Misses cost a flare, then a pirate, then an extra cannon shot.
  • After a win, choose Next Game to keep your streak and play again.
